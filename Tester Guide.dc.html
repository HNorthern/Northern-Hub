Northern Hub

How to open the system, protect your work, and move it safely across updates. Please read section 2 before you enter any data.

1. Getting started

Northern Hub runs entirely in your web browser. There is nothing to install.

  1. Open the link you have been sent in Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop. Phones and tablets are not supported for this pilot.
  2. Bookmark the page — you will return to the same address each time.
  3. Click Start Project Setup and enter your project's contract particulars.
Use fictional names. During the pilot, please do not enter real client names, real subcontractor names, or genuinely sensitive contract values. Use realistic shapes of numbers so the testing is meaningful, but keep identities anonymous.

2. Your data lives on your computer

This is the most important thing to understand. Your work is saved inside your own browser, on the machine you are using. It is not on a server and nobody else can see it.

This means Consequence
Your work stays on one machineOpening the link on a different computer shows an empty system
Clearing browsing data deletes itDo not clear cookies or site data for this address
Private/Incognito windows do not saveAlways use a normal browser window
Nobody else can see your projectsSend a backup file to share your work
Back up weekly, and always before an update. A backup is the only copy that survives a cleared cache, a new laptop, or a software update. The system will remind you, but the habit is yours.

3. Taking a backup

The backup controls are at the bottom of the left sidebar, beneath your name.

  1. Click Back up.
  2. A file downloads, named something like Northern-Hub-3-projects-backup-2026-08-16.json.
  3. Save it somewhere you will find it again — your OneDrive folder is ideal, not your Downloads folder.

One backup file contains every project you have created, together with all cost reports, variations, site instructions, certificates and cashflow profiles.

The sidebar shows when you last backed up. It turns amber, and a banner appears at the top of the screen, once your last backup is over a week old.

4. Send your backup in every Friday

Because your work sits on your own machine, nobody else can see it — including the company roll-up. So that the Commercial Director can see the overall position across all projects, we need your backup file once a week.

1
Every Friday afternoon, click Back up in the sidebar.
2
Email the downloaded file to Harry Newbegin, subject line Northern Hub backup — [your name].
3
Keep your own copy as well — sending it does not remove it from your machine.

Send the file even if you have not done much that week. A quiet week is useful information, and it keeps the company figures current.

5. When an update is released

You will be told when a new version is available. Follow these four steps in order.

1
Back up first. Before you do anything else, click Back up and save the file.
2
Hard-refresh the page. Hold Ctrl and press F5. Without this your browser may keep showing the old version.
3
Check the version. The number at the bottom of the sidebar should match the one you were told to expect.
4
Check your data is still there. Your projects should appear as normal. If anything is missing, use Restore — see section 6.

In most cases your data carries across an update automatically. The backup is insurance, not an expected step.

6. Restoring from a backup

Use this if data is missing after an update, if you move to a new machine, or if something has gone wrong.

  1. Click Restore in the sidebar.
  2. Choose your most recent backup file.
  3. The sidebar confirms how many projects were restored.
  4. Check a cost report and a cashflow to satisfy yourself the figures are right.
Older backups still work. A backup taken on a previous version is upgraded automatically when you restore it. You will never be locked out of your own data by an update.

7. What we would like you to test

Please work through a project as you would in reality, rather than clicking around. In particular:

  • Set up a project and build out the cost report with real-shaped packages
  • Place orders, hold contingency, and release packages you do not procure
  • Raise client variations through the full approval and agreement cycle
  • Issue site instructions, both recoverable and non-recoverable
  • Raise payment applications and subcontractor certificates, and take them through approval
  • Profile the cashflow and check it reconciles to the cost report

We are looking for anything that is wrong, anything that is slower than your spreadsheet, and anything that is missing from how you actually work.

8. Reporting a problem

When something looks wrong, please send:

  • What you were doing, step by step
  • What you expected, and what actually happened
  • A screenshot — press Windows + Shift + S
  • The version number from the sidebar
  • A backup file, if the problem involves specific figures
